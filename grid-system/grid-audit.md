# Nairoreel Grid Audit

Current grid: 12 posts across 4 rows. Reading left to right, top to bottom.
Audit maps each post to a treatment level, action, and rescue approach.

---

## Row 1 (top row)

### Post 1 — Cinematic Portrait (dark, moody)
- **Content:** Dark, atmospheric portrait. Blue-black tones. Film-grade feel.
- **Colour distance:** LOW — almost already on brand
- **Action:** KEEP
- **Treatment:** Universal base only (border + grain, filter Low)
- **Notes:** Best post on the grid. Sets the benchmark.

### Post 2 — "Reimagining Business Education" Poster
- **Content:** Bright teal/cyan background, bold graphic poster with a person and bold headline typography
- **Colour distance:** HIGH — teal is completely off-brand
- **Action:** RETIRE TO STORIES or Treatment A
- **Treatment if kept:** Treatment A (border frames it) + filter High + desaturation. The teal will still fight. Prefer retiring.
- **Notes:** This is a client deliverable / event-style poster. Not representative of the studio's visual work.

### Post 3 — Golden Hour Portrait
- **Content:** Warm golden/amber tones, person in hat against sunset. Beautiful image.
- **Colour distance:** MEDIUM — warm amber is off the cool dark brand palette
- **Action:** KEEP — strong image, rescuable
- **Treatment:** Universal base + filter Medium. Slight desaturation of the warm tones pulls it in without destroying the image.
- **Notes:** Could anchor a warm-toned row if paired intentionally.

## Row 2

### Post 4 — Documentary / Workspace
- **Content:** Person at a desk/workspace, warm interior lighting, candid documentary feel
- **Colour distance:** MEDIUM — warm interior tones
- **Action:** KEEP
- **Treatment:** Universal base + filter Medium. Desaturate slightly.
- **Notes:** Strong behind-the-scenes content. Pairs well with Post 1 if placed in a dark documentary row.

### Post 5 — Reload Can (stormy sky)
- **Content:** Reload energy drink can product shot, dramatic stormy sky, monochrome-ish background. Clean and cinematic already.
- **Colour distance:** LOW — dark, dramatic, already close
- **Action:** KEEP
- **Treatment:** Universal base only, filter Low. This one is almost there already.
- **Notes:** One of the stronger posts for grid cohesion.

### Post 6 — Reload Branding (bright green/lime)
- **Content:** Reload product on bright lime/green background with lemon/lime fruit. Very saturated.
- **Colour distance:** HIGH — lime green is maximally off-brand
- **Action:** RETIRE TO STORIES or split into Reload row
- **Treatment if kept:** Treatment A + filter High + heavy desaturation (Level 2). Even then it's a stretch.
- **Notes:** If Reload is a client the studio wants to show, build a 3-post Reload row with proper brand treatment across all 3 so the row-level identity compensates. Otherwise retire.

## Row 3

- **Colour distance:** MAXIMUM — this is a raw event flyer

### Post 8 — Silhouette Portrait (red/magenta)
- **Content:** Strong silhouette of a person, deep red/magenta wash. Very dramatic.
- **Colour distance:** LOW-MEDIUM — red is in the brand palette (#EA1821 adjacent)
- **Action:** KEEP
- **Treatment:** Universal base + filter Low. The red actually works. Let it breathe.
- **Notes:** Strong visual. Could anchor a Dark Red campaign row.

### Post 9 — Nguka Valentines Poster
- **Content:** Event/promo graphic, bright red background, "NGUKA #Payless Valentines" text heavy, very graphic design poster
- **Colour distance:** MEDIUM-HIGH — the red is brand-adjacent but the poster graphic style clashes
- **Action:** RETIRE or Treatment A
- **Treatment if kept:** Treatment A — the border frames it as a studio deliverable. Filter Medium. The red background actually makes this easier than most.
- **Notes:** Better than the Easter Praze poster — at least the colour is closer. Treatment A could work if Nguka is a client the studio wants on the grid.
