### KFC Drive-Thru — new upload
**Title:** `KFC Drive-Thru — 3D Product Animation | Nairoreel Productions, Nairobi`
**Description:**
```
A fully 3D-animated product commercial for KFC's drive-thru experience — a
self-initiated spec ad, not a paid client project, made by Nairoreel Productions
in Nairobi, Kenya.
https://nairoreelproductions.com

Built entirely in Blender at the end of 2024 as the studio's first full production,
then edited and sound-designed in DaVinci Resolve. Heavier renders were offloaded to
Google Colab's cloud GPUs since the studio's own laptops couldn't handle them. The
world is deliberately low-poly and cubic — closer to a miniature diorama than a
photorealistic simulation.

**Description:**
```
A 3D product animation for Reload's isotonic drink, made as a showcase and
